# Veya Experience — Public Website P1

## Status

Approved implementation direction for the public website. This describes a future product concept, not live medical functionality.

## Positioning

Veya is **your everyday intelligence companion**: there when useful and able to keep chosen context connected over time. It is the proposed human-facing daily relationship layer of Vascurra.

The experience must feel calm, optional and flexible. A person might use Veya once, several times or not at all on a given day. Availability must never be represented as constant observation, required reporting or surveillance.

## Relationship model

Veya may support:

- a quick check-in or everyday question;
- a chosen reminder;
- recording a note, routine, observation or priority;
- preparing questions for a future conversation or appointment;
- remembering source-aware context across time;
- surfacing themes for discussion and human review.

The person controls what is remembered, removed, shared, paused or left unused. Family access is permission-led. Clinician views remain conceptual and subject to human review. Research participation and personal-data use require a separate, explicit, governed process.

## Medication boundary

Veya may be described as helping a person remember and record an agreed medication schedule, retain its source and timing, or prepare medication questions for a clinician.

Veya must not be described or depicted as prescribing, changing a dose, stopping medication, recommending an independent change, maintaining the authoritative medication record or making an independent safety judgement.

## Safety boundary

Veya is not a doctor, diagnosis, treatment, medication prescriber, emergency service or replacement for clinical judgement. Patterns can be surfaced for review; they must not be converted into diagnoses or predictions. Urgent concerns should lead to deterministic signposting to appropriate emergency or clinical services.

## Page composition

The dedicated `/veya` page uses one H1 and ten editorial chapters:

1. A day with Veya.
2. Flexible interaction and conversation.
3. Memory and continuity.
4. Provenance.
5. Patterns for review.
6. Medication and routines.
7. Permission-led perspectives.
8. Veya and Vascurra Lab.
9. Safety and boundaries.
10. Person-controlled permissions.

The page belongs to the unfinished V2 surface and inherits `noindex, nofollow` from the V2 route layout.
