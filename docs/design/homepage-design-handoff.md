# Vascurra Homepage Design Handoff v1

## Authority
This document and the referenced assets are the design source of truth for the public Vascurra homepage.

The existing deployed homepage is **not** the visual source of truth. Cursor should implement the approved design rather than inventing a new one.

### Primary visual target
`/public/vascurra/reference/homepage-master-target.png`

### Canonical brand mark
`/public/vascurra/brand/brain-icon.png`

Do not redraw, regenerate or reinterpret the brain mark.

## Global art direction
- Predominantly pure white backgrounds.
- Deep Vascurra teal/navy only for deliberate cinematic sections.
- Teal, cyan and green are accents, not wallpaper.
- Generous white space and large visual compositions.
- Product-led, editorial and clinically credible.
- Avoid generic SaaS cards, tiny SVGs, pale tinted section backgrounds and repeated decorative waves.
- The brain icon appears only in navigation, hero and final CTA.

## Visual rules
1. Every major section needs a strong visual idea.
2. Small generic SVGs cannot be the primary visual content of a major section.
3. White space is a core part of the identity.
4. Vascular-flow artwork is an accent, not a background treatment for every section.
5. Do not repeat the brain icon throughout the middle of the page.
6. Product mockups must be large enough to feel premium and legible.
7. Do not flatten the whole page into screenshots; recreate layout/copy in HTML/CSS and use imagery where it is genuinely visual content.
8. Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior and performance.

## Visual QA
After each section is implemented, compare the rendered browser screenshot side-by-side with the listed reference. Do not proceed to the next major section until the current one reaches comparable visual quality.

Recommended sequence:
1. Hero + whole-person section
2. Four Perspectives
3. Understand / Track / Support
4. Privacy
5. Dark capability section
6. Responsible development
7. Final CTA
8. Mobile refinement

## Medical/product guardrails
The references contain illustrative concept UI. Do not present fictional scores, clinical validation, certifications, research findings or medical functionality as live/validated product claims. Preserve the design language while using truthful development-stage content.
