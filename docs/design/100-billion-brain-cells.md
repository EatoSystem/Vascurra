## Scientific provenance

Verified 4 October 2026:

- Azevedo et al. (2009), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19226510/ : adult male
  brain sample mean 86.1 billion neurons; this is not an exact universal count
  and does not count all brain cells. The campaign target is symbolic.
- NHS causes, https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vascular-dementia/causes/ : reduced
  blood flow and brain tissue damage.
- NHS treatment, https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vascular-dementia/treatment/ :
  currently no cure; treatment and support still have a role.

AI and compute are described as proposed tools, without a comparative scale
claim, clinical-effectiveness claim or promised prevention/treatment outcome.
Population-growth statistics and unsupported diagnoses/detection claims are omitted.
