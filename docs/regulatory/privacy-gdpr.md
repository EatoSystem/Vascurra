# Privacy & GDPR — Ireland/EU-First Working Principles

## Status

Product-development guidance only; formal privacy advice and a DPIA will be required before processing health data.

## Phase 1 public website

Data minimisation is the default.

The early-access form should collect only information needed to manage interest, for example:

- name;
- email;
- role/category;
- country/region if genuinely useful;
- communication consent.

Do not ask visitors to describe diagnoses, symptoms, medications or health histories.

## Future health-data product

Before collecting special-category health data, document:

- controller/processor roles;
- lawful basis;
- special-category condition;
- purpose limitation;
- data minimisation;
- retention periods;
- data-subject rights;
- correction/export/deletion processes;
- sub-processors;
- international transfers;
- EU data residency/processing choices;
- security controls;
- incident-response process;
- research-reuse basis and consent model;
- DPIA.

## Privacy architecture principles

- separate identity from health observations where appropriate;
- encrypt in transit and at rest;
- least-privilege access;
- visible role permissions;
- immutable/appropriate audit history for material access;
- avoid storing raw conversation/audio by default without purpose;
- define deletion behaviour explicitly;
- do not use production health data for model training without an approved legal/ethical basis.

## Public copy rule

Do not claim `GDPR compliant` as a marketing badge simply because the site has a privacy policy. Describe concrete practices instead.
