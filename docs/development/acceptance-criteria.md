# Phase 1 Acceptance Criteria

## Functional

- all required routes render;
- navigation works on mobile and desktop;
- early-access form validates and submits;
- success/error states are accessible;
- no sensitive health fields are collected;
- metadata and social-preview images exist.

## Visual

- brand-consistent white / teal / cyan / mint design;
- no text overlap or clipping;
- no text baked into essential raster graphics;
- responsive type scales;
- visual rhythm consistent across pages;
- gradients/glow remain restrained and legible.

## Accessibility

- semantic headings;
- keyboard usable;
- focus visible;
- alt text provided;
- forms labelled;
- contrast acceptable;
- reduced-motion experience available.

## Performance

- optimised images;
- animation does not dominate bundle/load time;
- reasonable Lighthouse performance on mobile;
- no unnecessary third-party scripts.

## Claims and safety

- no prohibited claims from `intended-purpose-and-claims.md`;
- no fabricated evidence, scores, testimonials or certifications;
- future concepts labelled appropriately;
- footer/disclaimer consistent.

## Engineering

- TypeScript strict;
- lint passes;
- tests pass;
- production build succeeds;
- no secrets committed;
- significant product decisions reflected in docs.
