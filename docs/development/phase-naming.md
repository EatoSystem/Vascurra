# Vascurra Phase Naming

This hierarchy is the current naming convention. Historical documents may retain earlier terms such as “Phase 1A”; the decision log records how those names map to the current convention.

## Public Platform

- **Phase P0 — Holding page:** the limited public introduction and private preview gate.
- **Phase P1 — Public website:** the public information, privacy and early-access experience described in `docs/product/phase-1-website-spec.md`.

## Vascurra Product Platform

- **Phase 0 — Engineering and governance foundation**
- **Phase 1 — Patient 0 prototype**
- **Phase 2 — Personal experience**
- **Phase 3 — Secure longitudinal foundation**
- **Phase 4 — Veya**
- **Phase 5 — Family**
- **Phase 6 — Clinician**
- **Phase 7 — Vascurra Intelligence**
- **Phase 8 — Vascurra Lab**
- **Phase 9 — Research infrastructure**

The Public Platform phases do not authorize Product Platform implementation. Public designs and conceptual mockups do not authorize health-data collection, authentication or live clinical functionality.
