# Content & Voice

## Voice

Vascurra is:

- calm;
- precise;
- intelligent;
- respectful;
- hopeful without hype;
- clinically aware without pretending to be a clinician;
- clear about uncertainty.

## Never patronise

Avoid language such as:

- “simple enough for seniors”;
- “the elderly” as a default identity;
- “sufferers”;
- infantilising memory-game language;
- fear-based descriptions of inevitable decline.

Prefer:

- people living with cognitive change;
- people and families;
- independence;
- support;
- meaningful change;
- informed conversations.

## Claims language

Use:

- `designed to support`;
- `proposed`;
- `concept`;
- `may help organise`;
- `intended to provide context`;
- `future product direction`.

Avoid without evidence:

- `proven`;
- `prevents`;
- `predicts`;
- `detects disease earlier`;
- `reduces strokes`;
- `slows dementia`;
- `clinically validated`.

## Person-first brand copy

Good:

> Vascurra is being designed to help people remain independent, informed and connected as cognition changes.

Avoid:

> Vascurra manages dementia patients with AI.

## Clinician copy

Good:

> A concise, source-linked longitudinal summary designed to support better clinical conversations.

Avoid:

> AI identifies the diagnosis and recommends the next treatment.

## Research copy

Good:

> Vascurra aims to make important evidence easier to understand and, in later validated phases, support ethically governed research collaboration.

Avoid invented numbers, study names or partnerships.
