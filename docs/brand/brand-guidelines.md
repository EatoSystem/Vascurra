# Vascurra Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence

**Calm intelligence for vascular cognitive health.**

Vascurra should feel sophisticated, human, trustworthy and quietly futuristic. It must not feel cold, alarming, institutional or patronising.

## Primary lines

**Vascurra**

**AI for vascular cognitive health.**

**Support independence. Preserve identity.**

## Logo

The Vascurra brain icon combines:

- brain form;
- vascular/neural branching;
- flowing ribbon structure;
- luminous nodes;
- teal/cyan/mint energy.

Do not distort, recolour randomly, crop tightly or place over visually noisy backgrounds.

## Colour direction

Core palette direction:

- Deep Navy — body copy / authority.
- Teal — intelligence / trust.
- Cyan — energy / connection.
- Mint / Emerald — growth / support.
- Soft White — breathing space / clarity.

Exact production values should be stored as design tokens once the final website UI is implemented.

## Futuristic “shine”

Display headings may use a restrained multi-stop text gradient inspired by the icon:

`mint → teal → cyan → deep blue`

Rules:

- use primarily for large headings and selected emphasis;
- body text remains high-contrast solid navy;
- never sacrifice readability for glow;
- avoid excessive bloom/neon;
- keep gradient direction consistent within a page.

## Background language

Preferred:

- white or very light cool canvas;
- faint translucent waves;
- subtle vascular branches;
- soft gradients;
- generous negative space.

Avoid:

- dark cyberpunk backgrounds;
- saturated neon panels;
- generic hospital blue everywhere;
- visual clutter.

## Cards and UI

- soft borders;
- subtle shadows;
- large radii;
- translucent or white surfaces;
- strong information hierarchy;
- icons with restrained glow;
- no tiny text over imagery.

## Photography

If photography is introduced:

- real, respectful adults;
- natural environments;
- no stock “confused elderly person” clichés;
- no fear-based dementia imagery;
- represent independence, family connection and normal life.

## Typography

Use a highly legible modern sans-serif with excellent web rendering.

Requirements:

- clear distinction between display, heading and body sizes;
- comfortable line height;
- no ultra-light body weights;
- generous mobile sizing;
- avoid text embedded inside raster images for final production.

## Design review question

Does the page feel like an intelligent health platform **and** remain easy to read for someone experiencing cognitive change?
