# Public Origin Story Decision — 2026-09-07

## Decision

The founder has explicitly approved a narrowly scoped public disclosure for the Vascurra project origin story.

The public website may state only the following personal facts about the founder's father unless a later written decision expands this scope:

- he is the founder's father;
- he is a retired GP;
- he is living with early vascular dementia;
- Vascurra is being developed with and for him;
- he is contributing his perspective as both a person living with the condition and a retired doctor to the design of the project.

This approval exists so the authentic origin and purpose of Vascurra can be explained publicly. It is an editorial disclosure decision, not a general health-data authorization.

## Not approved for public disclosure

Do not publish or commit, without a new explicit decision:

- his name;
- date of birth or age;
- home or precise location;
- photographs or other identifying media;
- medications;
- test results, scores, measurements or medical records;
- clinician names or appointment details;
- personal transcripts;
- additional diagnoses or medical history;
- raw Patient 0 data.

## Data and product boundary

This decision does not authorize storage of real participant health data in the public repository, use of the marketing holding-page gate as authentication, production clinical AI, or any Patient/Family/Clinician/Research portal containing real health information.

All existing privacy, security, clinical-safety, claims and future-product architecture requirements remain in force.

## Release boundary

The V2 origin-story sections remain behind the existing holding-page gate while under development. The public holding page remains the Production experience until explicit V2 launch approval is given.
