# User Journeys

## Visitor journey — Individual

1. Lands on Vascurra homepage.
2. Understands that Vascurra focuses on vascular cognitive health.
3. Sees independence and identity rather than fear-based dementia messaging.
4. Explores Personal concept.
5. Understands what Vascurra is not.
6. Registers interest without sharing medical data.

## Visitor journey — Family member

1. Recognises the challenge of changes between appointments.
2. Learns about consent-based observation and care-circle concepts.
3. Sees that family access is not assumed or invisible.
4. Registers as a family design partner or early-access user.

## Visitor journey — Clinician

1. Immediately sees conservative claims language.
2. Understands the proposed GP Visit Pack / longitudinal-summary concept.
3. Sees source provenance, no diagnosis, no prescribing.
4. Reviews science and regulatory approach.
5. Registers as a clinician design partner.

## Visitor journey — Researcher

1. Understands the small-vessel / vascular cognitive health focus.
2. Reviews evidence methodology and research governance principles.
3. Sees that research reuse requires ethics, consent and governance.
4. Registers interest in collaboration.

## Visitor journey — Funder/partner

1. Understands problem, product architecture and founder story.
2. Reviews responsible business model.
3. Sees staged validation rather than premature clinical claims.
4. Makes contact through a partner pathway.
