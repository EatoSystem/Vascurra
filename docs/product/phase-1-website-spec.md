# Phase 1 Website Specification

> **Current phase name:** Public Platform Phase P1. Historical references to “Phase 1” in this document mean the public website, not Product Platform Phase 1 (Patient 0 prototype).

## Objective

Launch the public foundation of Vascurra at `vascurra.com`.

The website should establish trust, explain the problem and product vision, introduce the four connected experiences, communicate safety boundaries and collect early-interest registrations.

## Primary audiences

1. People interested in vascular cognitive health.
2. Family members and care partners.
3. GPs, geriatricians, neurologists and allied professionals.
4. Researchers and academic partners.
5. Potential design partners, funders and investors.

## Required pages

### `/`
Homepage.

Sections:

- animated Vascurra brain hero;
- `AI for vascular cognitive health`;
- `Support independence. Preserve identity.`;
- problem overview;
- Personal / Family / Clinical / Research overview;
- founding philosophy;
- how Vascurra is different;
- safety and trust section;
- science/evidence approach;
- early-access CTA.

### `/about`

- why Vascurra exists;
- project principles;
- clinical co-design approach;
- Dr. Joe Curry — retired GP, development lead and clinical co-designer;
- founding-family story at a level that preserves private health information.

### `/personal`

Conceptual future experience:

- voice-first daily support;
- Recall → Hint → Guide → Answer;
- routines and orientation;
- health education;
- appointment preparation;
- identity-based conversation;
- explicit statement that Phase 1 does not provide live medical functionality.

### `/family`

- care-circle concept;
- natural observations;
- weekly summaries;
- consent and permissions;
- support without surveillance.

### `/clinical`

- GP Visit Pack concept;
- longitudinal summary principles;
- facts vs inference;
- provenance;
- clinical-boundary statement;
- regulatory review note.

### `/research`

- evidence library vision;
- research-watch concept;
- clinical-trial awareness as future functionality;
- research governance principles;
- no fabricated study metrics.

### `/science`

- vascular cognitive health explainer;
- evidence methodology;
- what the product can and cannot claim;
- sources only when verified.

### `/privacy`

- privacy philosophy;
- Ireland/EU-first approach;
- consent, capacity and control principles;
- public-site privacy notice once analytics/forms are configured.

### `/early-access`

Interest form with role selection:

- Individual
- Family member
- Clinician
- Researcher
- Partner / funder

Collect the minimum information necessary. Avoid collecting health details in Phase 1.

## Optional pages

- `/vision` — public project overview.
- `/news` or `/journal` — only if there is a sustainable editorial process.

## Navigation

Recommended:

`Platform | Personal | Family | Clinical | Research | Science | About`

Primary CTA: `Join early access`

## Hero motion

Preferred sequence:

1. soft particles gather;
2. vascular branches grow;
3. teal/mint ribbons form;
4. brain silhouette resolves;
5. nodes pulse softly;
6. Vascurra wordmark appears.

Requirements:

- reduced-motion fallback;
- no autoplay audio;
- fast page performance;
- static fallback image;
- no visual claim of diagnosis/prediction.

## Phase 1 data

Permitted:

- analytics;
- simple interest form;
- role selection;
- email updates consent.

Not permitted without a new approved scope:

- medical history;
- diagnosis details;
- medication data;
- glucose readings;
- symptom journals;
- patient documents;
- clinical records.

## Success criteria

- premium brand presentation;
- clear proposition understood by first-time visitors;
- no misleading health claims;
- WCAG-conscious implementation;
- strong Lighthouse performance;
- responsive across mobile/tablet/desktop;
- early-access form works reliably;
- every public statement can be traced to approved source copy.
