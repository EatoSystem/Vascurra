# Product Architecture

## One platform, four perspectives

Vascurra is conceived as four connected experiences sharing a consented information foundation.

## 1. Vascurra Personal

Future direction:

- natural voice conversation;
- orientation and routines;
- memory scaffolding;
- personal preferences;
- appointment preparation;
- health-information explanation;
- identity-based activity and conversation.

Signature interaction:

**Recall → Hint → Guide → Answer**

The level of assistance should be configurable rather than always giving an immediate answer.

## 2. Vascurra Family

Future direction:

- natural-language observation logging;
- care-circle coordination;
- longitudinal summaries;
- appointments and practical updates;
- explicit access controls;
- change awareness without default passive surveillance.

## 3. Vascurra Clinical

Future direction:

A clinician-readable summary designed around five questions:

1. What changed?
2. When did it change?
3. What else changed?
4. What is verified and what is reported?
5. What may warrant review?

The product must not turn those questions into autonomous diagnosis or prescribing.

## 4. Vascurra Research

Near-term public website:

- evidence education;
- methodology;
- future research vision.

Long-term, subject to ethics and governance:

- trial awareness;
- approved research collaboration;
- de-identified datasets;
- patient-reported outcomes;
- validated digital measures.

## Shared future foundation

- identity and preferences;
- permissions and consent;
- structured observations;
- provenance and timestamps;
- explicit medical context;
- longitudinal summaries;
- audit trails;
- export and correction rights;
- provider-independent AI layer.

## Phase 1 implementation note

These are **product concepts**, not functionality to be built into the public site as real health workflows.
