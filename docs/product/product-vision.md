# Product Vision

## Vision

Vascurra aims to become a trusted intelligence layer for vascular cognitive health: helping people remain independent and informed, helping families understand meaningful change, helping clinicians see useful longitudinal context, and helping research learn responsibly from consented real-world experience.

## Core proposition

**AI for vascular cognitive health.**

Vascurra should not begin from the assumption that a person needs to be managed. It should begin with the person: their identity, routines, capabilities, relationships, goals and preferences.

## North-star principle

> **Support independence. Preserve identity.**

The product should support existing capability before compensating for loss. It should scaffold rather than unnecessarily replace cognition, judgement or daily function.

## Who Vascurra serves

### Personal
A calm, voice-first support experience for everyday orientation, organisation, memory scaffolding, education and appointment preparation.

### Family
A consent-based care-circle experience that turns natural observations into useful longitudinal context without creating surveillance.

### Clinical
A concise, source-linked view of meaningful change between appointments, designed to improve conversations rather than automate clinical judgement.

### Research
An evidence and learning layer that begins with research education and trial awareness and could later support approved, consented research collaborations after ethics, governance and validation.

## Product philosophy

1. **Preserve identity.** A person is more than a diagnosis.
2. **Support independence.** AI should scaffold before it substitutes.
3. **Respect intelligence.** Cognitive change must never justify patronising design.
4. **Use longitudinal context.** Trends can be more useful than isolated snapshots.
5. **Separate facts from inference.** Source provenance must remain visible.
6. **Keep humans responsible.** Vascurra supports decisions; it does not autonomously make medical decisions.
7. **Design privacy and consent first.** Access should be understandable, visible and revocable where legally possible.
8. **Validate before claiming impact.** Product ambition must not outrun evidence.

## Long-term opportunity

The first focus is vascular cognitive health. Future expansion into adjacent cognitive-health use cases should happen only after the core model is validated and the regulatory implications are understood.

## Phase 1 interpretation

The public website communicates the vision but must not present future concepts as existing validated functionality.
