# Intended Purpose & Claims Guardrails

## Status

Working product definition for Phase 1. This is **not** a regulatory determination.

## Phase 1 intended purpose

The Phase 1 website provides information about a proposed support platform for vascular cognitive health and allows interested people, families, clinicians, researchers and partners to learn about the concept and register interest.

It does not deliver medical functionality.

## Future product intent — working hypothesis

Vascurra may eventually help individuals and their support network capture, organise and review information over time and prepare for informed conversations with clinicians.

The exact intended medical purpose must be reviewed before regulated functionality is designed.

## Claims permitted in Phase 1

Examples:

- `AI for vascular cognitive health.`
- `Support independence. Preserve identity.`
- `A proposed human-centered support platform.`
- `Designed to help people, families and clinicians organise meaningful context over time.`
- `Built with privacy, consent and human judgement in mind.`

## Claims prohibited unless formally evidenced and approved

Do not claim that Vascurra:

- diagnoses vascular dementia;
- diagnoses small-vessel disease;
- predicts cognitive decline;
- predicts stroke;
- detects disease before symptoms;
- prevents stroke;
- prevents dementia;
- slows progression;
- improves cognition;
- improves survival;
- reduces hospitalisation;
- changes medication safely;
- identifies a treatment plan;
- is clinically validated;
- has a validated risk score;
- is a medical device unless/when formally classified and marketed as such;
- has regulatory certification unless achieved.

## Prohibited visual claims

Do not use unexplained scores such as:

- `82/100 Brain Health`;
- `Vascular Age`;
- `Low Risk` / `High Risk` outputs;
- invented probability percentages.

Mockups may show neutral concepts such as:

- observations logged;
- timeline;
- source documents;
- reported changes;
- questions for review;
- consent status;
- appointment preparation.

## Copy approval

Any new healthcare outcome claim should be treated as blocked until evidence, regulatory and legal review are complete.
