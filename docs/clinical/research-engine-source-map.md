# Research Engine scientific source map

Reviewed on 6 October 2026. These references support background framing and
research domains only. They do not endorse Vascurra, establish its capabilities
or approve a study. The programme architecture and all capital figures come
from the founder's brief and approved repository data, not the research sources.

Public source records live in `content/research-engine.ts`. Each programme's
`sourceIds` links to those records. A blank list means a founder-proposed design
or future capability, not an unsupported public finding. Any new factual
research claim needs a source and limitations recorded here before publication.

## Additional methodological checks

- [Free-water MarkVCID validation study, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9745638/): original research in defined cohorts. It illustrates the need for reproducibility and validation; no performance number or validation claim is transferred to Vascurra.
- [PlGF/free-water multimodal study, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11848340/): original cross-sectional research. Its temporal and causal limits informed the wording “relate over time” as a future research question, rather than an established Vascurra finding.
- [SPRINT MIND, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/): randomised trial with a specified hypertensive population and distinct cognitive endpoints. Its primary probable-dementia outcome was not statistically significant; this site makes no universal prevention claim and reproduces no treatment recommendation.

| Experimental medicine and therapeutics | Proposed programme; `ukDri` | Hypotheses need appropriate preclinical/experimental/clinical validation; no prescribing, cure or treatment-benefit promise. |
| Trial Intelligence | Proposed methods research | Simulation is not clinical evidence; no trial matching, recruitment or enrolment. |
| Cure Programme | Proposed long-term therapeutics research | Aspirational name with adjacent boundary; competing hypotheses may fail and priorities must follow evidence. |
| Capital horizons and full mission | Proposed capacity planning; shared Brain Cells data | Resources cannot guarantee discoveries, timelines, revenues or health outcomes; €10B is separate from four horizons. |

Before any real programme, appropriate vascular-cognition specialists,
neuroradiology/MRI-methods experts, laboratory and assay scientists,
epidemiologists, biostatisticians, trialists and ethics/privacy/governance
specialists must review the relevant construct, methods and intended use.
The website's source review is not that clinical or scientific approval.
