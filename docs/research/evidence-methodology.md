# Evidence & Research Methodology

## Purpose

Vascurra should separate evidence communication from marketing claims.

## Phase 1 research content

Permitted:

- educational explainers;
- summaries of reputable guidelines/reviews;
- clearly cited public research;
- explanation of uncertainty;
- description of future research questions.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, where appropriate:

1. clinical guidelines / consensus statements;
2. systematic reviews and meta-analyses;
3. randomised controlled trials;
4. well-designed cohort studies;
5. other observational evidence;
6. mechanistic/preclinical evidence;
7. expert opinion.

The website should not present lower-level evidence as established clinical truth.

## Research-watch content

Every research item should include:

- source;
- publication date;
- study type;
- population;
- what it found;
- what it did not establish;
- relevance to Vascurra.

## Future product validation

Recommended progression:

### Stage 1 — usability and desirability

- adoption;
- ease;
- dignity/respect;
- family burden;
- clinician usefulness;
- trust;
- safety.

### Stage 2 — prospective pilot

Formal consent, defined inclusion criteria, oversight and pre-specified outcomes.

### Stage 3 — clinical evidence

Only after product and data model stabilise should Vascurra investigate disease-specific outcomes, predictive models or regulated decision support.

## No fabricated research

Mockups and public site content must never invent study names, partners, publications or metrics.
