# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1 objective

Do not “launch a dementia app”. Build credibility and recruit the right design partners.

## Ireland-first pathway

### Audience 1 — Families and individuals

Message:

- independence;
- identity;
- everyday support;
- better preparation;
- no fear-based selling.

CTA: join early access / co-design community.

### Audience 2 — Primary care and specialists

Message:

- clearer longitudinal information;
- less noise;
- source-linked summaries;
- support, not autonomous clinical decisions.

CTA: become a clinician design partner.

### Audience 3 — Research and academia

Message:

- vascular cognitive health focus;
- rigorous evidence approach;
- potential for ethically governed longitudinal research.

CTA: research collaboration discussion.

### Audience 4 — Innovation/funding ecosystem

Message:

- Ireland-founded health-AI platform;
- clinically informed co-design;
- staged regulatory strategy;
- global problem with an Ireland-first development model.

## Phase 1 channels

- vascurra.com;
- founder/clinical network;
- selected professional outreach;
- research partnerships;
- innovation programmes;
- thoughtful long-form content.

Avoid broad paid consumer acquisition until product utility and claims strategy are validated.

## Positioning

Do not position as:

- a cure;
- a diagnostic app;
- a generic brain-training app;
- a carer surveillance tool.

Position as:

**a human-centered intelligence and support platform for vascular cognitive health.**
