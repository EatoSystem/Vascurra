# Funding Strategy

> The current public financing direction is governed by the 2026-09-22
> capital-mobilisation decision in `docs/operations/decision-log.md`.

## Objective

Build a permanent, diversified capital and resource flywheel that matches the
maturity and evidence level of Vascurra while preserving founder, legal and
strategic flexibility.

Vascurra is not being designed around one funding round, one research grant,
one investor or one programme. Equity financing is neither prohibited nor
assumed.

## What mission capacity can include

- cash capital;
- philanthropy, grants and funded research;
- AI model access, compute credits and cloud infrastructure;
- clinical, scientific, engineering, legal and ethical expertise;
- university, research and healthcare-system collaboration;
- technical and in-kind contributions;
- future commercial and institutional revenues;
- licensing where appropriate;
- longer-term permanent or endowment-style structures where legally and
  strategically appropriate.

Mission capacity is cumulative. It does not mean the same amount of cash is
held at one time.

## Public capital horizons

1. **€1–5M — Foundation:** Patient 0, Veya, the core information system and
   research infrastructure.
2. **€10–25M — Research Engine:** Vascurra Lab, AI, compute, governed cohorts,
   evaluation and Family/Clinical development.
3. **€50–100M+ — International Network:** multi-country research,
   infrastructure and collaboration.
4. **€1B+ — Permanent Global Capacity:** cumulative long-term financial,
   technical and research capacity.

These are illustrative strategic planning horizons. They are not current
funding, announced equity rounds, valuations, commitments, secured resources or
guarantees.

## Phase 0–1

Preferred:

- founder capital;
- Irish/EU innovation grants;
- feasibility funding;
- health-research collaboration funding;
- philanthropic support where aligned;
- in-kind clinical/research partnerships.

Goal: minimise premature dilution while intended purpose, regulatory path and product utility are still being discovered.

## Phase 2

Potential sources:

- paid design-partner pilots;
- research grants;
- strategic healthcare partners;
- seed investment if product and regulatory strategy are coherent.

This is one possible future source, not a default financing decision or a
currently announced plan.

## Phase 3+

Potential:

- institutional venture funding;
- health-system contracts;
- research partnerships;
- international innovation programmes.

## Funding-readiness milestones

Before material external investment, aim to have:

- strong Phase 0 findings;
- regulatory opinion;
- privacy architecture;
- working MVP;
- design-partner evidence;
- clear intended purpose;
- credible unit economics hypothesis;
- independent clinical advisory input.

## Grant/investor narrative

Lead with:

1. unmet problem;
2. founder/clinical insight;
3. disciplined safety boundaries;
4. human-centered product model;
5. Ireland-first validation pathway;
6. large long-term global relevance;
7. business model that does not rely on selling health data.

Public website language should normally use `capital mobilisation`, `mission
capacity`, `planning horizon`, `resource mobilisation` and `research capacity`
rather than venture-round terminology. It must not solicit investment, promise
returns, imply tax deductibility or charity status, or present prospective
sources as secured.
