# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Co-design
**0–4 weeks**

- founding-user workflow discovery;
- top intents;
- memory-scaffolding tests;
- clinician-summary concept;
- safety boundaries;
- regulatory opinion preparation.

## Phase 1 — Public foundation
**0–3 months**

- vascurra.com;
- brand system;
- product narrative;
- early-access recruitment;
- clinician/research partner outreach;
- privacy and regulatory groundwork.

## Phase 2 — Private prototype
**~3–6 months, subject to decisions**

Potential:

- voice support;
- structured observations;
- weekly review;
- controlled family permissions;
- prototype clinical summary.

Only after privacy, security and regulatory scope are approved.

## Phase 3 — Design partners
**~6–12 months**

- small controlled cohort;
- clinicians/families;
- formal evaluation;
- usability/trust/safety measures.

## Phase 4 — Formal pilot / clinical pathway
**~12–24 months**

- ethics/privacy/regulatory structure;
- integrations as justified;
- evidence generation;
- commercial validation.

## Phase 5 — Scale
**24+ months**

Only after clinical, regulatory, security and commercial model are proven.
