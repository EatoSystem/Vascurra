# Vascurra master roadmap

Updated 7 October 2026. Public Platform and Product Platform remain separate.
Supabase, OpenAI and Stripe are **activation dependencies, not current blockers**.

## Current authorised development sequence

1. Provider contracts, fixture adapters and fail-closed capabilities.
2. Credential-free synthetic Research → Evidence → Human Decision workstation.
3. Institutional memory / Atlas, with source, finding, interpretation, hypothesis
   and decision kept distinct.
4. Brain Cells mock participation, checkout outcomes and history.
5. Veya synthetic support and person-controlled context journeys.
6. Public story: Veya supports the person; VeyAI assists research and operations;
   Brain Cells help fund capability; Vascurra Research turns questions into learning.
7. Non-executable Operations and Capital proposal interfaces.

Each slice requires lint, typecheck, tests and a production build. UI slices also
need browser review. Completion and validation are recorded in the provider
architecture document; this sequence is not a claim that all items are finished.

## Activation later

Supabase verified identity/MFA/RLS/persistence → bounded OpenAI Research, then
Evidence → Stripe after legal, commercial, tax, accounting and refund review.
The clinical product, real personal information, autonomous external actions,
programme execution and capital allocation remain deferred. Veya fixture context
must never flow automatically into VeyAI institutional research.
